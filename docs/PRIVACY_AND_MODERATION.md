# GovChain Privacy & Content Moderation Policy

## 1. Privacy Principles
- **Public Transparency, Private Identity**: Public records showcase civic issues, department statuses, and budgets. Private citizen contact info, email addresses, phone numbers, and home coordinates are never exposed in public feeds.
- **Location Generalization**: Exact residential addresses are never broadcasted; only civic landmarks, ward names, and approximate coordinates are rendered on public maps.
- **Anonymous Tracking**: Citizens can track submitted reports and grievances via high-entropy tracking tokens (e.g. `ISS-2026-00001`, `GRV-2026-00001`) without authenticating.

## 2. Content Moderation & Abuse Prevention
- **Defamation & Personal Data**: Reports containing unverified personal accusations, phone numbers, or residential addresses are flagged for human review.
- **Preservation of Legitimate Criticism**: Government actions and civic failures are freely reportable. Moderation actions require documented reasons.
- **Duplicate Merging**: When multiple residents report the same physical pothole or pipe burst, a moderator merges them into a parent issue. All previous citizen comments, support counts, and timestamps are preserved in the ledger.
